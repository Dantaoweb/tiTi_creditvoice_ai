Questions

Frequently asked

What is CreditVoiceai?

A financial assistant called tiTi that helps small and medium businesses record sales, track who owes them, manage stock and send receipts — with a dashboard for reports that becomes a credit scorecard over time.

Do I need to download an app?

Is it free?

Yes — the Basic plan is free. Paid plans add unlimited records, staff accounts, branches and more.

What can tiTi do?

Record cash and credit sales, track debtors and send reminders, manage inventory with low-stock alerts, generate receipts and invoices, show reports and profit, and support staff, branches and partners.

Is my business data safe?

Your records are yours. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act, and nothing is shared with a financing partner unless you choose to apply and agree to share it.
