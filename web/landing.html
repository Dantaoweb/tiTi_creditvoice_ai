Frequently asked questions

What is CreditVoiceai?

CreditVoiceai is a WhatsApp assistant called tiTi that helps small and informal businesses record sales, track who owes them, manage stock, and send receipts — with a web dashboard for reports.

Do I need to download an app?

No. You can use CreditVoiceai entirely inside WhatsApp by chatting with tiTi. There is also a web dashboard and an Android app, but no download is required to start.

Is CreditVoiceai free?

Yes, there is a free Basic plan. Paid plans (Go, Pro, Premium) add unlimited records, staff accounts, branches and more.

What can tiTi do?

Record cash and credit sales, track debtors and send reminders, manage inventory and low-stock alerts, generate receipts and reports, and support staff, branches and partners.
